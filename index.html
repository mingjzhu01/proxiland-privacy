Proxiland Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Proxiland is a location-based professional networking app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It's written in plain language and reflects how the app actually works today.

Information we collect

  • Account information: the email address you sign up with.
  • Profile information: your name, photo, role, industry, seniority level, education, employer, job title, bio, what you're looking for, what you can offer, hometown, and similar details you choose to add to your profile.
  • Location: your device's precise location, but only while you have "visibility" turned on. Visibility expires automatically after the time window you choose.
  • Messages: the content of chat messages you send to people you're connected with.
  • Connection activity: requests to connect or reveal your identity, including any message you include, and where and when you connected (for example, at which event).
  • Events: if you join an event, your membership, whether you're checked in, your "ask & offer" answers for that event, and your match results.
  • Push notification token: a device identifier used to deliver notifications, if you allow them. This is not personal information on its own.
  • Basic usage events: app activity tied to your account (like opening the app or sending a request), used to keep the app reliable. We use only our own first-party logging for this — no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs are used in the app.
  • LinkedIn (optional): if you choose to connect your LinkedIn account, we receive basic profile information from LinkedIn to verify your identity.

How your location is used

Your precise coordinates are used only on our servers to determine who is nearby, and are never shown to other users. Other people only ever see a distance description like "in this building" or "nearby" — never an address or exact position.

Details about you (like your school or employer) only appear on the anonymized card shown to a nearby stranger if enough other people nearby would also match that same detail — this keeps any single fact from narrowing down who you are.

How you appear to others

Before you've connected with someone, they see a limited, anonymized version of your profile — never your name or photo. Your full identity is shared with someone only when you ask to connect with them, or when you both agree to reveal yourselves to each other.

AI processing

Proxiland's AI features are provided by Anthropic, a third-party AI provider. To make them work, some of your information is sent to Anthropic for processing:

  • Your profile (such as your name, role, employer, education, and bio) — to structure your profile fields, write and polish your headline and bio, and explain what you have in common with another user.
  • At events, your "ask & offer" answers together with your profile — to match you with other attendees and explain each match. Other attendees' profiles and answers are processed the same way when they are matched with you.

We ask for your permission in the app before any of this happens. When you first sign in, Proxiland explains this processing and asks you to agree before any AI feature runs. We don't sell this information or use it for advertising.

The optional "Draft with AI" feature is different: it only runs when you tap it, and it performs a web search using the name, school, and employer you've entered, specifically to try to find and confirm your real public identity before writing a draft bio from what it finds. Any draft is shown to you for review — nothing is saved to your profile unless you approve it. The search results themselves are not stored on our servers.

Who we share information with

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising. We share information only with the service providers that make the app work:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, file storage, and user accounts.
  • Anthropic — processes profile and event information for the AI features described above, with your permission.
  • Resend — sends transactional emails, such as internal notifications when a report is submitted.
  • Expo, Apple, and Google — deliver push notifications to your device.
  • LinkedIn — only if you choose to verify your profile through LinkedIn.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. You can permanently delete your account at any time from within the app (Profile → Delete account) — no need to contact us. This removes your profile, photos, messages, connections, and device tokens.

One nuance worth stating plainly: if you've submitted a report about another user, that report record is deleted along with your account too. However, if we already received an email notification about that report before you deleted your account, that email may still exist in our inbox — deleting your account doesn't reach into already-sent email.

Your rights and choices

You can review and edit most of your profile information directly in the app at any time. You can turn location visibility on or off whenever you like. You can block or report another user from their profile, from a chat, or from an anonymized card. To delete your account, use the in-app option described above. For anything else — including questions about your data — contact us at the email below.

Children

Proxiland is intended for professionals aged 17 and older. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 17.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
